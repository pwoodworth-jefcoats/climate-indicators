## North Pacific Gyre Oscillation

**About the Indicator**  
The North Pacific Gyre Oscillation (NPGO) is related to, but distinct
from, the Pacific Decadal Oscillation. It capture basin-scale processes,
making it well suited to capture change across the Hawaiʻi-based
longline fishing grounds

Understanding the effects of natural climate variability like the NPGO
on the ocean, marine ecosystems, and fisheries is an active area of
research.

**Status of the Indicator**  
The NPGO was negative in 2024. The index ranged from -2.5 to -1.44 over
the course of the year. This is within the range of values observed
previously in the time series.

**Indicator Data**  
Description: The NPGO is driven by regional and basin-scale changes in
wind patterns. Changes to the wind field drive changes in ocean
circulation that affect nutrients and, in turn, phytoplankton. Because
phytoplankton are the foundational food source for marine ecosystems,
the NPGO offers insight into potential ecosystem change. Description
paraphrased from Di Lorenzo et al. (2008).

Timeframe: Monthly.

Region/Location: 180°–110°W; 25°N–62°N.

Measurement Platform: satellite, model.

Data available at: <https://www.o3d.org/npgo/data/NPGO.txt>.
